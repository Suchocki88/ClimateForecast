What do you notice?
Reading the data: Temperature changes are in °C relative to the 1850–1900 average. Each value is the IPCC's assessed best estimate for a 20-year period, not the temperature in a single year. SSP1-2.6 is a low-emissions scenario; SSP5-8.5 is a very-high-emissions scenario. These are conditional projections, not predictions of what people will choose to do.

Source: IPCC, Climate Change 2021: The Physical Science Basis, Working Group I, Summary for Policymakers, Table SPM.1. IPCC AR6 report.
